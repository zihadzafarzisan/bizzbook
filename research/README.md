# Bizzbook research

Direction: a Facebook-like, all-category business social network for Bangladesh. Manufacturers, wholesalers, and entrepreneurs publish posts, discover opportunities, follow businesses, and communicate through DMs.

1. [Initial research](bizzbook-bangladesh-research.md): social-commerce context, local and international references, product hypotheses, and initial validation proposal.
2. [Round 2](bizzbook-research-round-2.md): close historical precedent, adoption evidence, buyer retention, network relevance, trust, and evidence gaps. This qualifies the initial report's conversation metric.
3. [Field research guide](field-research-guide.md): proposed interviews, Bangla prompts, concept testing, pilot measurement, and decision criteria.

All prepared on 15 November 2026. This is desk research and a proposed fieldwork plan. No interviews, outreach, competitor-account testing, or customer-demand validation have been performed. Sources and access limitations appear in the reports. No application implementation is included.
